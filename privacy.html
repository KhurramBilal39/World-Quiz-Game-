World GK Quiz Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

What we collect

World GK Quiz does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools in the app.

What stays on your device

The app saves your best score on your own device so it can show it the next time you play. This data never leaves your device, and it is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Third parties

The app does not send your data to any third party.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
