Test what you know about the world in quick 10-question rounds.

World GK Quiz mixes questions from geography, science, history, sports, culture and technology, with a few on Pakistan. Every round is different, because questions are picked at random from a growing pool.

HOW IT WORKS
- 10 questions per round, 20 seconds for each
- Answer fast to earn bonus points
- Build a streak by getting answers right in a row
- See the correct answer and a short fact after every question
- Review the questions you missed at the end of the round
- Your best score is saved on your device
- Works offline, with no sign-up needed

No ads. No account. No tracking.
